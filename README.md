# ZEPARI Policy Hub

Design a complete, modern and visually distinctive website for ZEPARI, a Zimbabwean economic policy research institution, using https://zepari.co.zw/ as the primary brand and content reference.

Use these websites for visual and editorial inspiration only:
- https://acetforafrica.org/
- https://www.brookings.edu/africa-growth-initiative/
- https://saiia.org.za/

Create a clean, authoritative, contemporary African research-platform aesthetic with sophisticated typography, generous whitespace, editorial layouts, refined colours based on ZEPARI’s existing branding, subtle animations and authentic photography of researchers, workshops and policy engagements. The design must remain distinctive to ZEPARI and its Zimbabwean identity.

**PAGES & NAVIGATION**
Build all main pages and relevant subpages: Home, About, Research, Insights, Policy Dialogue, Media, Publications and Contact. Include the required team, governance, research areas, policy briefs, working papers, explainers, blogs, events, news, videos, podcasts, reports and newsletters sections. Getting all the exact content from the website.

**HOMEPAGE**
Create a compelling editorial hero, featured research, latest insights and news, research themes, upcoming events, media highlights, newsletter signup and clear calls to action. Make research easy to discover and communicate why ZEPARI’s work matters to Zimbabwe and the wider region.

**RESEARCH & CONTENT**
Create consistent, reusable templates for research items, publications, articles, research themes, events and media. Research pages must support abstracts, authors, dates, key findings, policy recommendations, related resources and downloadable files. Add searchable, filterable publication archives with keyword, year, research area and publication-type filters. Connect related content automatically through reusable CMS records.

**PAID PUBLICATIONS**
Design a secure pay-per-item purchase journey for selected articles and publications—no recurring subscription requirement. Visitors should see metadata and a free preview, select a price, complete checkout and access protected full text or downloads only after confirmed payment. Include free/paid content settings, purchase confirmation, account access and clear download/view options. Structure the design to support payment gateway integration, including suitable Zimbabwean and international payment methods. Do not expose protected files through public URLs.

**VISUAL DIRECTION**
Make the website feel like a premium economic policy institute: intellectually engaging, credible, human and digitally sophisticated. Use strong editorial typography, excellent content hierarchy, attractive research cards, data visualisations where appropriate, thoughtful Zimbabwean/African imagery and subtle motion. Avoid clutter, generic templates and excessive colour blocks.

**FUNCTIONALITY**
Make all navigation, filters, search, forms, social links, sharing actions and CTAs functional. Include LinkedIn, X, Facebook and YouTube links, plus a newsletter form that can connect to ZEPARI’s chosen mailing service.

Build reusable CMS-friendly structures so ZEPARI staff can manage research, publications, prices, authors, events, media and related content without duplicate entry.

Deliver a complete, responsive, accessible design across desktop, tablet and mobile. Prioritise a striking visual identity, effortless research discovery, clear editorial storytelling and a trustworthy, secure publication-purchasing experience.
